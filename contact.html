Common Questions

Where is my order?
Orders ship within 3–5 business days of placement. You will receive a tracking email once your order ships. Check your spam folder if you haven't received it.
How do I become a creator?
Sign up at our Creator Signup page. It's free and takes under 15 minutes to upload your first design and publish your store.
When do creators get paid?
Creator earnings are paid out on a bi-weekly schedule. You can view your pending and confirmed earnings in your earnings dashboard.
Can I return or exchange a product?
Yes — InkForge offers free returns within 30 days for products that arrive damaged, defective, or significantly different from the listing. Contact us with your order number and we'll sort it out.
