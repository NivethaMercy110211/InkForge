Why We Exist

Our Mission

InkForge exists to remove every barrier between an artist's creative vision and a living income. We believe that great design deserves a direct path to the people who will love it.

In 2022, our founders — two designers frustrated with the complexity of merchandise logistics — built InkForge as the platform they wished existed. A marketplace where creators set their own prices, own their audience relationships, and see transparent real-time earnings data.

Today, InkForge serves 2,400+ creators across 60 countries, has facilitated over 180,000 orders, and has paid out more than $2 million in creator earnings.

How We Got Here

Our Journey

From a weekend side project to a platform serving thousands of creators worldwide — the InkForge story is one of persistence, community feedback, and a genuine love for original design.

2022 to 2026
Four years of growth, innovation, and global creator empowerment
Q1 2022
InkForge Founded
Two designers with a shared frustration launch InkForge as an invitation-only beta. First 50 creators onboarded. First 200 orders fulfilled.
Q3 2022
Public Launch
InkForge opens to all creators. 400 sign up in the first week. The product catalog expands to include mugs, tote bags, and phone cases alongside t-shirts.
Q1 2023
Seller Dashboard 2.0
Full-featured seller dashboard launches with real-time analytics, earnings charts, and CSV export. Creator satisfaction scores jump 40%.
Q3 2023
International Shipping Expansion
InkForge now ships to 60+ countries. International orders grow to 35% of total volume. New print partner facilities in Europe and Asia added.
Q2 2024
1,000 Active Creators
Milestone: InkForge reaches 1,000 active creator accounts. Total earnings paid exceed $1 million. Hoodies and poster prints added to the catalog.
2026
2,400+ Creators and Growing
InkForge continues to grow. 50,000+ designs available. $2M+ paid to creators. The mission remains the same — put creators first.
