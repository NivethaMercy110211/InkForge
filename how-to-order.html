How to Order

From browsing to doorstep — your complete ordering guide.

Complete Guide

Placing an Order — Step by Step

InkForge makes it simple to discover, personalize, and order custom merchandise.

01

Browse & Discover

Use the Products page to filter by category, style, or creator. Search for specific designs using keywords.

02

Customize Your Order

Select product type, color, size, and quantity on the product detail page. Choose between available variants.

03

Add to Cart

Add items to your cart and keep shopping. Your selections stay saved while you review product details, quantities, and options before checkout.

04

Secure Checkout

Enter your shipping and payment details securely. We accept major cards, PayPal, and InkForge credit.

05

Printed on Demand

Your item is printed fresh after your order — no pre-made stock. Production takes 1–3 business days.

06

Delivered to You

Your order ships and arrives within the estimated window. Track via email or your order confirmation link.

Helpful Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about delivery, sizing, changes, and returns.

How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping takes 5–8 business days after production (1–3 days). Express shipping (2–3 business days) is available at checkout. International orders typically arrive within 10–14 business days.

What sizes are available?

Apparel sizes range from XS to 4XL depending on the garment. Each product page shows available sizes. A size guide link on the product page provides measurements in both cm and inches.

Can I cancel or change my order?

Orders can be cancelled or modified within 12 hours of placement, before production begins. Contact support@inkforge.com immediately with your order number.

What is your return policy?

We accept eligible returns within 30 days for defective, damaged, or incorrectly listed items. On-demand products cannot be returned for size preference changes.

Do you ship internationally?

InkForge ships to more than 60 countries worldwide. International rates and estimated delivery times are displayed at checkout based on your destination, selected service, and order.
