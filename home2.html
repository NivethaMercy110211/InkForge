Step by Step

Your Journey as a Creator

From your first upload to your first payout — here's exactly how it works.

01
Step One

Create Your Artwork

Design your artwork in any software you prefer — Illustrator, Procreate, Photoshop, or even hand-draw and scan. InkForge accepts PNG, JPEG, and SVG formats at high resolution.

  • Minimum 150 DPI for apparel, 300 DPI recommended
  • PNG with transparent background for best results
  • SVG supported for scalable vector artwork
  • No minimum artistry level — all styles welcome
02
Step Two

Choose Your Products

Select which products you'd like to offer your design on. Apply it to t-shirts, hoodies, mugs, tote bags, phone cases, posters, caps, and more.

  • Apply one design across multiple product types
  • Choose available sizes, colors, and variants
  • Set different prices for different products
  • Preview exactly how your design will look
03
Step Three

Preview Your Merchandise

See realistic mockups of your design applied to every chosen product. Adjust placement, scale, and positioning before publishing. What you see is what your customers receive.

  • Realistic product mockups generated instantly
  • Adjust print position and scale
  • Preview in different product colors
  • Review before any commitment
04
Step Four

Publish Your Store

Write your product description, add tags, set your selling price, and go live. Your creator profile and product listings are immediately discoverable by InkForge shoppers.

  • Custom creator profile with portfolio
  • Searchable product listings
  • Featured in relevant category pages
  • Share your store link anywhere
05
Step Five

We Print on Demand

When a customer orders your product, we handle everything. Our print partners produce the item fresh — direct-to-garment, sublimation, or digital print depending on the product type.

  • Professional print quality on every order
  • Multiple printing methods for different products
  • No stock to manage or warehouse needed
  • Automated from order to production
06
Step Six

Orders & Fulfillment

Monitor incoming orders in your seller dashboard. Track fulfillment status in real time. Customer support and returns are handled by InkForge — you can focus entirely on creating.

  • Real-time order tracking in your dashboard
  • Automatic fulfillment notifications
  • Customer support handled for you
  • Returns and replacements managed by InkForge
07
Step Seven

Earn & Get Paid

Your earnings are the difference between your selling price and the base production cost. Track earnings in real time. Payouts are processed on a regular schedule to your preferred payment method.

  • You set your own selling price
  • Earn = Selling price − Base production cost
  • Transparent earnings dashboard
  • Regular payout schedule — no waiting months
